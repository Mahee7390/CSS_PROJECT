A little about us

A borrowed umbrella, ten years and two thousand kilometres

It started in Pune, on a rainy afternoon when one borrowed umbrella turned a small conversation into a friendship.

Life took us to different cities. There were long phone calls, train journeys, airport hugs and weekends planned around seeing each other.

Somehow, every journey brought us closer. Now, Udaipur will be the place where our favourite story begins its next chapter.

We can't wait to celebrate with you.
